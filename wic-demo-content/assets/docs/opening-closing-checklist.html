Clinic Opening and Closing Checklist (Sample)

Sample document for demonstration. Your agency supplies the approved version.

Opening

  • Check the day’s schedule and any messages left overnight.
  • Check the equipment you need is working and in place.
  • Make sure the waiting area is tidy and signage is visible.

Closing

  • Secure paper records and lock every screen.
  • Note anything the next shift needs to know.
  • Tell your supervisor about anything unusual.
