Privacy Do’s and Don’ts — Quick Reference (Sample)

Sample document for demonstration. Your agency supplies the approved version.

Do

  • Lock your screen whenever you step away.
  • Collect printouts straight away.
  • Move to a private space for sensitive conversations.
  • Report a mistake to your supervisor as soon as you notice it.

Don’t

  • Open a record without a work reason.
  • Discuss someone’s details where others can hear.
  • Leave paper records in public view.
