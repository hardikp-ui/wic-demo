New Starter First-Week Checklist (Sample)

Sample document for demonstration. Your agency supplies the approved version.

  • Sign in to the training portal and complete “Using the Training Portal”.
  • Meet your supervisor and your mentor.
  • Sign your required forms under Forms.
  • Find the Library and read your clinic’s procedures.
  • Book your place on the next orientation session.
  • Write down any questions for your first check-in with your mentor.
